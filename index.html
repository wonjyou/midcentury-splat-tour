About this tour

The room is drawn entirely from 3D Gaussian splats — about half a million soft, oriented ellipsoids, each with its own colour and opacity, depth-sorted every frame and blended in your browser. The light is path-traced when the page loads: sun and lamp shadows, sky light through the glass, and one bounce of colour off the walls and rug. Glossy surfaces pick up highlights as you move. In Live view, a face model running locally finds your eyes, and the view uses an off-axis projection so the screen behaves like a pane of glass you're looking through. Turn your head and the view turns with you. Without a camera, your pointer stands in for your head.

Keyboard: ← → to move between spots, R to recenter.
